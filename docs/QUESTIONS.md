# OPEN QUESTIONS

These are prompts for collaborative design, not a checklist to answer all at once.

## Fantasy
- Are we heroic, morally grey, villainous, strange, cozy, grim, comic, tragic?
- Is the fantasy about people, creatures, constructs, spirits, or mixed beings?
- Is the player an embodied character, a commander, an organization, or an unseen force?

## Battle
- What does the player know before combat?
- What is hidden or uncertain?
- What can be changed before Commit?
- What can never be changed once the battle starts?
- How long should one automatic exchange be before the player acts again?

## Build expression
- What can combine: roles, elements, formations, relationships, equipment, rituals, tactics, timing rules?
- Can a build create new behavior, or only bigger numbers?
- What is one combo that would be exciting to watch even with placeholder art?

## Loss
- Does a failed fight end the run, injure units, consume time, lose resources, or create a new branch?
- Can a partial failure be interesting?

## Readability
- What three things must the player be able to read during a battle without pausing?
