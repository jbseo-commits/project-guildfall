# NARRATIVE VERTICAL SLICE — THE FIRST PULSE

> **Status: IMPLEMENTATION TARGET / PROVISIONAL NARRATIVE**
>
> This is the first playable narrative slice built on `docs/WORLD-BIBLE.md`.
> It should make the tutorial feel like the opening of the actual game, not a detached mechanics lesson.

---

## 1. Player-facing premise

**Night 1.**

The Living Caravan has just awakened strongly enough to be detected.

The party is moving through **Moonscar Wood** toward the **Moonwell**, hoping to stabilize the Heartseed before it becomes dangerous to the inhabitants.

A **Glass Directorate containment unit** has already found the trail.

The opening battle is therefore:

> **not “fight random monsters.”  
> It is “break through the first containment line before the Caravan is identified and boxed in.”**

---

## 2. Tutorial-to-story mapping

### READ — “They found us.”
Mechanical lesson:
- read enemy intent.

Narrative meaning:
- Directorate hunters are trained to expose the ecosystem's weak point.

Player takeaway:
- the enemy is not attacking randomly;
- they are trying to learn how the Caravan protects itself.

---

### PLACE — “Show them the wrong weakness.”
Mechanical lesson:
- formation.

Narrative meaning:
- the Keeper chooses which behavior the enemy is allowed to see first.

Vael can invite the obvious frontal attack.

Seris can remain protected long enough to complete the ritual.

Mirel stays close enough to whoever is likely to break first.

---

### COMMIT — “Now trust them.”
Mechanical lesson:
- surrender direct control.

Narrative meaning:
- the Keeper cannot puppeteer every movement;
- the Heartseed and the inhabitants must perform the plan.

This is the emotional point of autobattle.

---

### WATCH — “The Caravan answers.”
Mechanical lesson:
- observe behavior.

Narrative meaning:
- Vael protects because he recognizes Seris's danger;
- Mirel heals because she senses stress;
- Seris finishes because the others bought her time.

The fight should demonstrate that this group is already becoming an organism.

---

### DEBRIEF — “It learned us.”
Mechanical lesson:
- understand causality.

Narrative meaning:
- the Heartseed has recorded the relationship pattern.

This prepares the concept of Resonance.

---

### RESONANCE — “A habit becomes instinct.”
Mechanical lesson:
- pair two inhabitants to create a conditional automatic reaction.

Narrative meaning:
- repeated trust becomes part of the Caravan's nervous system.

---

## 3. Opening text budget

Do not use a large lore dump.

The first screen should establish only:

> **1일차 · 첫 맥동**  
> 월흔숲, 달우물로 향하는 길  
>  
> “심장씨앗이 깨어난 뒤 처음 맞는 밤.  
> 누군가 우리의 맥동을 들었다.”

Everything else should emerge through:
- enemy labels;
- character lines;
- route title;
- short tutorial copy;
- post-battle debrief.

---

## 4. Character lines

### Camp — before threat read

**Vael**
> “추적 흔적이 가까워졌어. 이번엔 내가 먼저 보여 줄게. 너희는 끝까지 준비해.”

Subtext:
He deliberately makes himself the obvious answer to the enemy.

**Seris**
> “달우물까지는 멀지 않아. 내 의식이 끊기지만 않으면 길을 열 수 있어.”

Subtext:
She needs the others and dislikes admitting it.

**Mirel**
> “심장씨앗이 또 떨고 있어… 우리 때문이 아니라, 밖에서 누가 건드리는 것 같아.”

Subtext:
She can feel the Caravan as a body.

---

### Battle dialogue — Seris

Before COMMIT:
> “저 추적자는 눈으로 보는 게 아니야. 맥동을 읽고 있어. 내가 노려지면… 부탁할게.”

During battle:
> “집중은 유지할게. 나머지는 너희를 믿을게.”

## 5. Enemy framing

The first enemy card should say:

### Glass Directorate · Containment Unit 7
**Mission:** identify the Living Sanctuary and immobilize its Heartseed.

Units:
- Crystal Breaker — front breach
- Blind Hunter — resonance target suppression
- Stigmatized Hound — wounded-target pursuit

This information should remain concise in the playable UI.

---

## 6. First debrief framing

Instead of:
> “You won because front tank reduced damage.”

Prefer:
> **The Caravan learned a pattern.**

Examples:

- **Vael exposed himself first**  
  The Breaker committed to the front.

- **Seris remained protected**  
  The Hunter's suppression failed.

- **Mirel recognized the damage pattern**  
  The weakest inhabitant was restored before collapse.

- **Time was created**  
  Seris completed the Moonlight ritual.

The debrief should connect mechanics to relationship behavior.

---

## 7. Resonance reveal

After the first battle:

> **The Heartseed remembered what happened.**

Then introduce Resonance:

> “When the same trust repeats, the Caravan begins to react before the Keeper asks.”

This converts the mechanic from a generic synergy feature into a world rule.

---

## 8. End hook

After the Resonance tutorial:

> **Destination: Moonwell**
>
> The Root Choir reacts to the approaching Heartseed.
>
> A voice that is not a voice reaches the Keeper:
>
> **“You came back wearing different names.”**

This ends the tutorial arc with a mystery rather than another mechanics card.

Do not explain it yet.
